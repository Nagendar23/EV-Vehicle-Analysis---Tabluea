# Electric Vehicle Analytics Dashboard — Tableau

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze the electric vehicle market and identify key trends in EV adoption, vehicle types, manufacturers, models, and geographic distribution.

## KPIs

* **Total Vehicles** — Overall number of electric vehicles in the dataset, including BEVs and PHEVs.
* **Average Electric Range** — Average electric driving range across the vehicles.
* **Total BEV Vehicles** — Number of Battery Electric Vehicles, along with their percentage of total EVs.
* **Total PHEV Vehicles** — Number of Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles, along with their percentage of total EVs.

## Charts & Visualizations

### 1. Total Vehicles by Model Year

**Visualization:** Line / Area Chart
Shows EV growth from **2010 onwards**, highlighting adoption trends and changes in the number of electric vehicles over time.

### 2. Total Vehicles by State

**Visualization:** Map Chart
Displays the geographic distribution of EVs across states and helps identify regions with higher EV adoption.

### 3. Top 10 Vehicles by Make

**Visualization:** Bar Chart
Ranks the top 10 manufacturers based on the number of electric vehicles, providing a view of market dominance.

### 4. Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility

**Visualization:** Donut / Pie Chart
Shows the distribution of vehicles based on **Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility**, providing insight into potential incentive eligibility.

### 5. Total Vehicles by Model

**Visualization:** Grid View
Highlights the **top 10 EV models** by vehicle count, helping identify the models with the strongest presence in the dataset.

## Objective

The dashboard transforms raw EV data into an easy-to-understand visual story, making it possible to quickly assess **EV growth, vehicle composition, market leaders, consumer preferences, geographic adoption, and CAFV eligibility**.

## Tools Used

* Tableau
* Electric Vehicle Datasetta Visualization
* Calculated Fields and KPI Analysis
